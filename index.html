My interests include flow matching, diffusion models, information-theoretic deep learning, and neural architecture search. I work on machine learning applications in physics, climate, and materials science.

Projects

Foundation Modeling for Astro

The goal is to develop state-of-the-art foundation models for joint modeling of multi-modal astronomical datasets. The problem involves pretraining and fine-tuning transformer models to learn interpretable embeddings across modalities.

Nuclear Fusion

The goal is to develop foundation models for detecting and mitigating the risk of plasma instability inside Tokamak reactors. The problem involves spatiotemporal generative modeling of the plasma field.
